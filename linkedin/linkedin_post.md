# ⚖️ Built RegulAIte: Intelligent Regulatory Compliance & Contract Risk Auditing

Complex agreements and evolving regulatory policies create massive operational blind spots for enterprises. To solve this, I developed **RegulAIte** — an automated legal and compliance intelligence engine that inspects agreements and identifies contractual liabilities before execution.

### 🛠️ What I Built & Deployed:
• **Automated Smart Document Review**: High-precision ingestion of agreements, commercial contracts, and compliance policies with clause-by-clause classification.
• **Dynamic Risk Gauge & Red Flag Scanner**: Real-time risk scoring, liability severity ranking, and immediate detection of unfavorable indemnification and governing law clauses.
• **Automated Remediation Roadmap**: Actionable clause replacement recommendations that align draft agreements with industry compliance benchmarks.
• **Modern Next.js Dashboard**: Re-architected with a modular frontend, custom KPI telemetry widgets, contextual audit trails, and legal form generation.
